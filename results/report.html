What is still wrong, and why

  • ny_01, ny_02 · issuing_authority. The document prints three stacked lines (The State of New York / Department of Professional Registration / Office of Licensed Professions); the expected value is a condensed rewrite. A rule that reproduces it exactly would be tuned to these two documents, so it is left as a known miss.
  • Fixed along the way. Dates in any printed format → ISO in code. Degree suffixes (MD, RN, DDS) stripped from names. expiration_status computed from the reference date, never by the model. Sideways pages rotated upright from the PDF text layer, which recovered the accent in LUCÍA on tx_01.
  • Caught by the eval. A first post-processing version raised on non-ISO dates and failed 6 of 8 documents (23.4%). A first wording of the credential_type rule regressed al_01 (93.8%). Both were visible before anything else changed.
Eval: python eval.py results/outputs/<run>/ --label <run>. Strings compared after trimming, whitespace collapse and case folding; dates exact ISO; null only matches null. Full write-up in results/RESULTS.md.
